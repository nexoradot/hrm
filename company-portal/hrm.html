COMPENSATION

Payroll workspace

Calculate monthly salaries and Kenyan statutory deductions, review payroll, approve it, print payslips and record payment references.
Monthly payroll calculates PAYE, NSSF Year 4 from February 2026 (Tier I: 6% up to KES 9,000; Tier II: 6% from KES 9,001 to KES 108,000; employee and employer contributions), SHA/SHIF at 2.75% of gross pay (KES 300 minimum), and employee/employer Affordable Housing Levy at 1.5%. Verify variable pay, benefits and deductions before approval. NEXORA records payment references but does not transfer funds or file statutory returns.

Payroll periods

Use the workflow action to advance periods. Approval and payment status changes are recorded in the database.

Employee payroll lines

Manual deduction inputs and calculated gross/net arithmetic for each employee.
